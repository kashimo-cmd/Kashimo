# K08 IMU and position

- Out: `pi/controller/imu.py`, `pi/controller/position.py`, `tests/test_position.py`, `tests/test_imu.py`.
- Position (5.6 steps 2-5): `integrate_gyro(gz, dt)` is `yaw += (gz - bias) x dt` (call at 100 Hz); `move(ds)` is x, y, s update plus the heading snap (call at 20 Hz); `update(ds, gyro_z, dt)` does both. `ds_from_tracks(prev, cur)` is the mean of both tracks, so a pivot adds 0. `ds` already carries cal_factor from K05, so it is not applied again. Yaw in degrees, 0 = direction when lowered, positive = left. A gyro of None or NaN (IMU LOST) freezes yaw, the path keeps counting.
- Snap (default OFF, config `imu.snap`, `snap_window_deg` 25, `snap_straight_m` 1.0): after 1.0 m with no turn above 3 deg/s, yaw goes to the nearest multiple of 90 only when within 25 deg; otherwise nothing changes. `move()`/`update()` return True when it was applied.
- Imu (5.6 steps 1, 4, 6, 7): status CALIBRATING, OK, UNCERTAIN, LOST. Boot: 3 s of a still robot gives the gyro bias; any movement (gyro spread over 2 deg/s, accel off 1 g by 0.05, encoder count) restarts the 3 s. Standstill (gyro under 1 deg/s, accel within 0.03 g, no encoder count for 1 s) pulls the bias slowly (10 s time constant [EST]). Tilt from gravity through a 0.5 s low-pass: WARN above 25 deg, ALERT above 35 deg, `pop_tilt_alert()` once per episode, re-armed under 25 deg. `pop_bump()` fires once at 0.5 g above rest (0.5 s cooldown [EST]). No valid sample for 0.5 s [EST] means LOST; back from LOST is UNCERTAIN, with no recalibration.
- UNCERTAIN means "heading uncertain": the heading was integrated since the last correction (calibration or snap). With snap OFF this shows as soon as the robot moves, as the spec says.
- K09 wiring: every 10 ms `imu.read(hw, moved=...)` then `pos.integrate_gyro(imu.heading_rate, 0.01)`; when `imu.pop_calibrated()` do `pos.bias = imu.bias` and `pos.reset()`; keep `pos.bias = imu.bias` every tick; every 50 ms `ds = ds_from_tracks(prev, cur)` and `if pos.move(ds): imu.note_correction()`. Drive gets `imu=imu.status`. `pop_bump()` becomes EVENT BUMP and `pop_tilt_alert()` EVENT ALERT_TILT, both with s, x, y.
- Tests: pivot adds 0 distance, L-shaped run (10 m, 90 deg, 5 m) and a quarter circle end within 1 %, snap inside and outside the window, calibration restart, standstill bias, tilt and bump thresholds, LOST. Synthetic samples and fake HW only. NOT RUN on the Pi.
- Not done (CHECK ON PI): MPU6050 axis directions (x forward, y left, z up assumed; pitch and roll signs follow from that), yaw 0 and Q13, the [EST] time constants, and the gyro bias drift over a 2 min drive.
